Made for gamers and streamers

It does not just cut at random. It looks for the moments people actually watch.

🔥
Finds the real moments

Loudness jumps, screaming, what is said ("oh my god", "bhaago"), fast action, explosions and a local AI read, combined into a 0–99 viral score.

💬
Hinglish captions

Hindi speech written in English letters, 2–4 words at a time, with the spoken word lit up. Five styles: GTA Neon, Hormozi, MrBeast, Impact, Clean.

🎮
Facecam layouts

Face on top, game below. Pick the webcam corner or draw your own boxes. Blurred fill and more.

📡
Live clipping

Paste a live Twitch or YouTube link. It records, watches chat speed and cuts a clip around every spike while the stream is on.

⭐

Pick your setup

Windows is one click. Everything else has an easy way too.

⬇ Download FreeClipping-Setup.bat
  1. Open the downloaded file (double-click). If Windows asks "Windows protected your PC", press More info → Run anyway. The file is plain text and not code-signed; you can open it in Notepad first.
  2. It checks your PC and installs what is missing (Python, FFmpeg). Needs internet, about 3–8 minutes, no admin rights.
  3. It asks two optional questions (GPU libraries, AI titles) and then opens FreeClipping in your browser.
  4. Next time just double-click the FreeClipping shortcut on your Desktop.
Everything is installed in one folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\FreeClipping). Delete it to uninstall.

Questions

Is it really free?+

Yes. It is open-source (MIT). No account, no subscription, no watermark, nothing locked behind a donation.

Does it upload my videos?+

No. Everything runs on your own computer. Videos, clips and your ratings stay in the app folder. The app only talks to YouTube/Twitch/Kick to fetch the video you paste.

What do I need?+

A Windows 10/11 PC (a Mac/Linux script exists but is untested) and internet for the first install. An NVIDIA GPU makes some steps faster but is not required.

Does it work on my phone?+

The tool runs on a computer. This website works on a phone, but the clip maker needs a PC. Using your phone as a remote for the app on your PC (same Wi-Fi) is planned, not built yet.

Why does Windows show a warning?+

The setup file is not code-signed (that costs money). It is plain text: right-click → Edit, or open it in Notepad, and read every line. The app itself is fully open-source on GitHub.

How good are the picked moments?+

It finds loud, energetic, quotable moments with fast action. It is a heuristic plus a small local AI, not magic: always watch a clip before posting. Your star ratings make it better at your taste.

How do I uninstall?+

Delete the FreeClipping folder in %LOCALAPPDATA% and the Desktop shortcut. Nothing else is changed on your PC (Python and FFmpeg stay if you had them).
